9
Image Upload & Integration

🎯 Today's Goal:

Add your personal images to bring your digital space to life.

⚠️ File Naming Rules:

Use NO SPACES in filenames. Use dashes: my-photo.jpg ✓ NOT my photo.jpg ✗

📸 Image Upload Process:

1 Verify images properly named (profile.jpg, mood1.jpg, etc.)
2 In Neocities, click "Upload" and select images
3 Edit index.html file - click "Edit" next to it
4 Look for this EXACT text: src="https://via.placeholder.com/150"
5 Replace the entire placeholder URL with just your filename: src="profile.jpg"
6 Find other placeholder images (like src="https://via.placeholder.com/200x120") and replace with mood1.jpg, mood2.jpg, etc.

✅ Image Integration Checklist:

📊 For Your Slideshow:

Screenshot your site with your personal images - this shows your visual identity!
